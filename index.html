Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about boat rental Kotor no license, yacht charter Tivat Porto Montenegro, and cruising regulations.

For vessels up to 15 HP (such as our Ranieri 19 self-drive runabout), no boat license is required under Montenegrin maritime law (аренда катера котор без капитана). We provide a thorough 15-minute briefing on controls, speed limits, and anchoring before departure. For larger speedboats and yachts over 15 HP, you either need a valid international skipper license (ICC/RYA) or you can book with our licensed local skippers.

Our yacht charter Tivat Porto Montenegro vessels depart directly from the prestigious Porto Montenegro marina docks. We also arrange direct embarkation at Kotor Old Town Marina, Luštica Bay Marina, Budva Old Town Port, or directly at the private jetty of your waterfront hotel or villa (One&Only Portonovi, Regent Porto Montenegro, The Chedi).

On every Boka Bay private boat tour (морская прогулка боко которская бухта), your charter fee includes the professional English & Russian speaking skipper, all fuel consumed for the planned route, drinking water, local wine or beer, towels, and snorkeling masks. There are zero surprise surcharges.
